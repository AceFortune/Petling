PETLING FIX 98 — WATER POLISH

- Dynamic water geometry from Fix 97 is unchanged.
- Added a second lily-pad-free version of every one of the 256 dynamic water configurations.
- Lily pads now appear on only a small stable minority of water positions instead of every tile.
- Lily distribution is based on tile position, so pads do not flicker in/out when neighbouring water changes.
- Removed the bright cyan/light-blue CSS fallback that briefly appeared while a dynamic pond sprite loaded.
- Existing water images are proactively cached when the Garden opens to further reduce visible loading.
- All existing project assets and gameplay systems remain intact.
