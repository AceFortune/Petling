Petling Version 14 — Garden Overhaul Fix 45

SIXTH CASINO GAME: GET DOWN MR PRESIDENT!

Implemented the user's original two-player card game as a full player-vs-AI Casino mode.

CORE RULES
- Standard 52-card deck plus two Jokers.
- Each side receives one King President plus five random cards.
- The six Army cards are shuffled face down into a 3x2 formation.
- Rock, Paper, Scissors is mandatory before play and genuinely determines first turn.
- Defensive Round turns draw one card and use it for Defence, Attack or Reserves.
- Maximum five Defence cards.
- Reserves cannot exceed a combined value of 13.
- Each side may discard/reset its Reserve pile once per game.
- Defence must be attacked before Army cards.
- Combat uses card value; matching value is enough to beat the target ("meets beats").
- A destroyed Army position forces a replacement draw on that player's next turn.
- Jack = Oracle: reveals an opposing Army card permanently, bypassing Defence.
- Joker = Assassin: destroys any chosen Army card immediately, bypassing Defence.
- Killing a King President ends the game immediately.

END GAME
- Begins when the draw deck is empty.
- Defence cards are paired and revealed simultaneously.
- Higher Defence card scores one point; tied pairs score no point.
- Compared Defence pairs are discarded; an unmatched extra Defence card remains.
- Defence-showdown winner chooses who takes the first End Game turn.
- If Defence scoring ties, the original RPS winner gets that choice.
- End Game attacks use surviving Army or Reserve cards.
- Oracle and Assassin retain their specialist functions.
- President is protected from being selected as an attacker until it is the final available card.

AI FAIRNESS / STRATEGY
- AI decision code never reads hidden player Army cards.
- AI knows only its own cards plus Army cards it has legitimately revealed with an Oracle.
- Hidden player Defence cards are targeted without reading their values.
- AI uses information-based strategy: known-value attacks, President kills when discovered,
  Oracle scouting, Assassin priority, reserve management, defence building, and efficient
  End Game card selection.
- Unknown targets are genuinely selected without peeking.
- The AI is trying to win but has no hidden-information advantage.

CASINO INTEGRATION
- Free Play plus optional 5g / 10g / 20g stakes.
- Paid victory returns 2x stake; defeat loses the stake.
- The game has no effect on Petling happiness.
- Closing the game returns seamlessly to the Casino.

INTERPRETATION USED FOR AMBIGUOUS EDGE CASES
- "Meets beats" is implemented as attacker wins on equal value.
- A Joker drawn during forced Army replacement gets its Assassin action first, then replacement continues.
- Ordinary attacking cards are spent/discarded after attacking.
- In the End Game, a used Army attacker leaves its Army position permanently; no replacement draws remain.
- The President may only attack as a last resort if no other Army/Reserve attacker remains.
