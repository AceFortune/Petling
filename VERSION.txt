FIX 87 — AQUARIUM BLANK SCREEN ON CLOSE
- Fixed Aquarium closing to a blank screen.
- Root cause: Aquarium manually removed #mg-overlay but did not clear the global petling-menu-open lock/scrim introduced by the menu occlusion system.
- Aquarium now exits through the shared closeOverlay(true) cleanup path, which removes the overlay, unlocks the page, removes the underlay blocker and re-renders the main Petling screen.
- Lake cleanup is performed if the Aquarium is closed while in the lake view.
- Aquarium view resets to the main tank for the next open.

FIX 88 — PRAISE / SCOLD HOLD CURRENT POSITION
- Praise and Scold now preserve the Petling's exact roaming position and facing direction before the UI refresh.
- The reaction animation plays wherever the Petling was standing instead of resetting/teleporting it to the centre.
- Petling remains stationary for about 2.2 seconds while the praise/scold reaction is visible.
- Normal autonomous roaming then resumes from that same position.
- Existing happiness, bond, affection, attention and scolding rules are unchanged.

FIX 89 — STARTER CRITTERS + PETLING GARDEN INTERACTIONS
- Mosskin is now a forgiving Level 1 grass visitor: plenty of ordinary grass is enough to attract it.
- Glimmerkin is now a forgiving Level 1 soil visitor: creating a broad dirt/soil patch is enough to attract it.
- Pebblekin is now a forgiving Level 1 water visitor: a modest pond is enough to attract it.
- These three act as an organic terrain tutorial; their longer-term residency requirements remain intact.
- Petling movement in the Garden now chooses real contextual destinations: grown plants, decorations, water and active resident critters.
- On reaching a destination the Petling stops there and visibly interacts instead of merely wandering past it.
- Added flower/plant sniffing, decoration inspection, pond watching/drinking, critter watching/greeting and quiet resting behaviours.
- Petling temperament and likes weight these behaviours so different Petlings use the same Garden differently.
- No teleporting is used; the Petling physically travels to its interaction target.

FIX 90 — GARDEN PLANT DISAPPEARANCE SAFETY
- Investigated all direct Garden tap paths that can remove plants.
- Terrain painting can no longer silently delete a plant when changing to incompatible terrain; occupied tiles must be explicitly cleared first.
- CLEAR is now protected by a two-tap confirmation on the same object within 2.2 seconds.
- After one successful removal, CLEAR automatically switches off so casual follow-up taps cannot continue deleting the Garden.
- Switching tools cancels any pending clear confirmation.
- Added a direct-tap mass-removal safety guard: if one tile action somehow causes more than one plant to disappear, the pre-tap tile state is automatically restored.
- Normal watering, planting, decoration placement, plant growth and deliberate critter feeding remain unchanged.

FIX 91 — AQUARIUM LAKE FISHING FINISH-LINE PRIORITY
- Fixed an edge case where the final REEL tap could fill the catch/progress bar and tension bar on the same frame.
- The old code checked snapped-line tension before completed catch progress, causing a visible full progress bar to sometimes result in failure.
- Reaching 100% catch progress now resolves immediately as a successful catch before tension failure is considered.
- Normal tension failure still occurs whenever tension reaches its limit before the catch bar is completed.
- Added an extra reeling-state guard to the passive fishing timer to prevent stale failure resolution.

FIX 93 — GARDEN HARD DELETION SAFETY
- Confirmed and fixed the reported critter-tap plant deletion bug.
- Root cause: tapping a visiting critter called tryFeedCritter(), which silently found and deleted a matching grown plant anywhere in the Garden.
- Tapping critters is now non-destructive and informational only.
- Legacy critter feeding no longer consumes/removes placed plants.
- Critter UI taps stop event propagation so they cannot fall through onto Garden tiles underneath.
- Added a hard invariant to gardenRemoveFootprintAt(): it refuses to remove any plant or decoration unless the CLEAR/remove tool is actively selected.
- Fix 90's two-tap confirmation remains in place, and CLEAR switches itself off after one successful removal.
- Therefore, during normal gameplay, deliberate CLEAR selection + confirmation on the target asset is the only interactive route for deleting placed plants/decorations.

FIX 94 — GARDEN PETAL ECONOMY + RENEWABLE HARVESTS
- Reintroduced Petals as a Garden-only currency.
- Existing saves receive 220 starting Petals once; new Gardens start with 220.
- All landscaping/terrain remains completely free, including grass, soil/dirt and pond/water placement.
- Plants/seeds and decorations now have balanced Petal prices based on unlock level, tags and physical footprint.
- Prices are visible directly in the Garden palettes; unaffordable entries are visually dimmed and cannot be selected/placed.
- Petals are spent only when placement succeeds, so invalid placement never charges the player.
- Fully grown plants are permanent renewable producers: tapping a ready mature plant harvests/sells its logical bounty for Petals without removing the plant.
- Fruit trees sell fruit; berry plants sell berries; vegetables/herbs sell crops; mushrooms sell caps; flowers sell blooms/cuttings.
- Harvesting starts an asset-appropriate regrowth timer. The plant remains fully grown and can be harvested again when its bounty returns.
- Ready mature plants display a small SELL indicator.
- Critter first visits award Petals, repeat visits award a smaller amount, and becoming a resident awards a substantial rarity-scaled Petal reward.
- Garden Level still controls unlocks; Petals control affordability; landscaping stays free so habitat experimentation cannot soft-lock the player.

FIX 95 — CRITTER BREEDING, MULTIPLE SPECIES & TRADING
- Resident species now track an owned population rather than being limited to one individual.
- A suitable resident species can attract a second wild individual, creating a breeding pair.
- Two adults of the same species are required before breeding is possible.
- Every species gets a breeding-readiness requirement derived from and strengthened from its existing resident habitat requirements.
- Once the pair and habitat requirement are satisfied, BREED becomes available.
- Breeding produces a baby and starts a rarity-scaled maturation/cooldown timer.
- Babies mature automatically over real time and become owned adults.
- Mature offspring can be sold for Garden Petals; selling offspring does not affect plants/assets.
- Adult resident critters can also be sold voluntarily for Petals with a confirmation prompt.
- Critter sale values scale by rarity. Mature offspring sell for 80% of normal adult value.
- Residents screen is now RESIDENTS & BREEDING and shows owned count, breeding requirement/readiness, baby timer, mature offspring and sale controls.

FIX 96 — CRITTER REQUIREMENT LEVEL CONSISTENCY
- Audited all 182 Critter V3 appearance, visit and resident requirements against the actual Garden Level unlock table.
- Found and corrected 228 requirement references that could ask for a terrain, plant, decoration or functional tag before it was available.
- Requirements now obey a hard balancing rule: every requirement needed by a critter is achievable at or before that critter's own minimum Garden Level.
- Early critters remain genuinely early-game rather than secretly depending on late-game assets.
- Dripdrop's Level 1 residency no longer requires the late-game Crystal Birdbath; it now uses a Level 1 Pond-compatible decoration.
- Replacements preserve each critter's habitat theme as closely as possible using assets already available at that level.
- Added a runtime requirement-unlock audit that warns developers if a future critter edit reintroduces an impossible level dependency.
- No critter names, rarity assignments, habitat assignments or artwork were changed.
