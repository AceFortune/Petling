PETLING FIX 102 — CRAPS

- Added CRAPS as a new playable Casino game.
- Three betting modes: Pass Line, Don't Pass and Field.
- Pass/Don't Pass use proper come-out and point phases.
- Field is a one-roll wager with 2 and 12 paying 2:1.
- Supports Free Play plus 5g, 10g and 20g wagers, matching the existing Casino.
- Uses the existing Gold economy and save system.
- Includes animated dice, point indicator, roll history, concise rules and clean Casino navigation.
- Closing Craps returns to the Casino rather than exposing the screen beneath.
