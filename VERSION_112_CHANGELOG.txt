PETLING FIX 112 — DAY OUT MINIGAMES
- Every Day Out activity is now a playable mini-game rather than a reward button.
- 24 distinct activity/game pairings across Beach, Forest, Carnival, Zoo, Park and Tavern.
- Beach: Wave Hop, Sandcastle Stack, Shell Match, Sunset Breath.
- Forest: Trail Choice, Scent Tracker, Root Climb, Picnic Pack.
- Carnival: Ring Toss, Ride Balance, Prize Claw, Light Memory.
- Zoo: Animal Spotter, Aquarium Follow, Feeding Sort, Keeper Watch.
- Park: Fetch Catch, Lily Hop, Roll-o-Meter, Quiet Together.
- Tavern: Tavern Plate, Bard Rhythm, Local Chat, Fireside Story.
- Mini-game performance affects the happiness/bond reward, but even a poor attempt still counts as shared time rather than punishing the Petling.
- Each activity remains one-use-per-outing, preserving the one-hour Day Out structure and one Day Out per real calendar day.
- Existing Fix 111 Day Out, Fix 110 Penny Pusher and Quest Director systems are preserved.
