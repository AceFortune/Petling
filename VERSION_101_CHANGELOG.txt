PETLING FIX 101 — NATURAL LILY PAD DISTRIBUTION

- Fixed lily-pad water variants clustering into a specific row/band.
- Root cause: the previous deterministic modulo operated directly on the linear tile index, which could form visible grid patterns depending on the Garden column count.

- Approximately 1 in 8 eligible water positions still contain lily pads.
- Distribution is scattered naturally across the whole Garden rather than forming rows or columns.
- Lily placement remains deterministic/stable: editing neighbouring water will not make existing pads randomly flicker around.
