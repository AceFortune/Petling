
BATCH ITEM 1 — MAIN PETLING ROAMING RESTORED

- Restored horizontal movement around the main LCD viewer.
- Sprite flips horizontally when changing direction.
- Movement is now combined with the personality/mood system.
- Petlings alternate between roaming, stopping, and hopping on the spot.
- Some movement bursts are faster.
- Temperament influences movement frequency and speed.
- Energy and happiness also influence roaming behaviour.
- Existing creature artwork is untouched.
- Existing mood/personality sprite animations remain active on the sprite itself.

BATCH ITEM 2 — HOT SPRINGS BACKDROP

- Added the user's approved daytime and evening hot-springs artwork as game assets.
- 07:00 through 18:59 local device time: daytime hot-springs backdrop.
- 19:00 through 06:59 local device time: evening/night hot-springs backdrop.
- Backdrop appears only while the Petling is at the hot springs.
- Petling remains centered and does not roam while at the spring.
- Petling retains a subtle gentle breathing animation while soaking.
- Existing hot-springs timer/gameplay effects are unchanged.

BATCH ITEM 3 — AQUARIUM WATER-SURFACE BOUNDARY

- Lowered the aquarium fish upper swimming boundary from 6% to 16% of the tank.
- Slightly raised the lower safety margin as well (maximum Y 92%).
- Tap-attraction targets are now clamped to the safe underwater zone.
- Shy-fish escape targets use the same underwater safety boundary.
- Fish can still swim naturally near the surface, but should no longer overlap the lid or appear outside the water.
- Fish sprites/artwork and swimming behaviours are otherwise unchanged.

BATCH ITEM 4 — WEATHER ICON LABEL

- The real-world weather icon in the header is now tappable/clickable.
- Tapping it shows a small label beside the icon with the current weather state.
- Labels include Sunny, Cloudy, Rain, Snow and Thunderstorms.
- The label fades away automatically after about 1.4 seconds.
- Uses the existing weatherState data; no weather-fetching logic was changed.
- Works with pointer/touch input on iPhone and normal click input in browsers.

BATCH ITEM 5 — PERSONALITY-BASED WEATHER REACTIONS

- Petlings can now occasionally react to the real-world weather shown by the existing weather system.
- Reactions are based on each individual Petling's existing Likes, Dislikes and Temperament.
- Examples:
  - Petlings that like puddles, rain smells, cool water or splashing tend to enjoy rain.
  - Petlings that dislike wet paws/cold weather tend to dislike rain.
  - Thunderstorm lovers/Bold/Fiery Petlings can enjoy storms.
  - Shy, Nervous or loud-noise-hating Petlings can be unsettled by thunder.
  - Fresh-snow lovers enjoy snowy weather, while cold-hating Petlings prefer staying inside.
  - Sunny-morning, warm-sand and sunbeam lovers react positively to sunshine.
- Weather reactions appear as occasional home-screen thought bubbles, not constant alerts.
- A cooldown prevents the same weather from dominating idle behaviour.
- Strong weather preferences also subtly affect roaming: happy-with-weather pets move a little more;
  unhappy-with-weather pets tend to slow down.
- Existing personality data is used; no new random weather-preference stat was added.

BATCH ITEM 6 — GENDER SYMBOL VISIBILITY

- Changed the small male/female symbol beside the Petling's home-screen name to black.
- No other text, colours, artwork or UI elements changed.

BATCH ITEM 7 — DAY COUNTER FIX

- The home-screen DAY counter now starts at DAY 1 instead of DAY 0.
- It advances by one each full 24 hours from the Petling's createdAt timestamp:
  Day 1 = first 24 hours
  Day 2 = 24-48 hours
  Day 3 = 48-72 hours, etc.
- The counter is clamped to a minimum of 1.
- No evolution timers or age-based gameplay systems were changed.

BATCH ITEM 8 — CASINO HUB

- Removed Roulette from the PLAY & GATHER menu.
- Added a new THE CASINO button on the main Petling screen alongside Garden and Aquarium.
- Added a dedicated Casino overlay/hub.
- Roulette now lives inside the Casino.
- Closing Roulette returns to the Casino hub rather than dropping straight back to the home screen.
- Existing roulette mechanics, bets, wheel odds, payouts and gold handling are unchanged.
- The Casino hub is deliberately expandable so more casino games can be added later without cluttering PLAY.

BATCH ITEM 9 — BLACKJACK & POKER

- Added Blackjack to The Casino.
- Blackjack supports 5g/10g/20g bets, Hit, Stand, dealer draw-to-17, blackjack, busts and pushes.
- Added Five-Card Draw Poker to The Casino.
- Poker supports 5g/10g/20g bets, dealing five cards, tapping individual cards to hold them, and drawing replacements.
- Poker evaluates Pair, Two Pair, Three of a Kind, Straight, Flush, Full House, Four of a Kind and Straight Flush.
- Both games use the player's existing Petling gold and save winnings/losses.
- Roulette remains unchanged.

BATCH ITEM 10 — SLOT MACHINE

- Added Slots as the fourth game in The Casino.
- Supports 5g, 10g and 20g wagers.
- Animated three-reel spin sequence.
- Weighted symbols: Cherry, Lemon, Bell, Star, Seven and Gem.
- Matching symbols have progressively larger payouts.
- Three Gems is the 35x jackpot; three Sevens pays 20x.
- Smaller two-symbol and cherry consolation wins are supported.
- Uses and saves the player's existing Petling gold.
- Roulette, Blackjack and Poker are unchanged.

BATCH ITEM 11 — TRAINING GROUNDS DAY/EVENING BACKGROUND
