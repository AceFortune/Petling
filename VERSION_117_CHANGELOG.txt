PETLING FIX 117 — DISCIPLINE VS SPOILING

- Reworked random Petling demands into a genuine choice system.
- When the attention/scold prompt appears, the player now gets two options: SCOLD or GIVE IN.
- SCOLD:
  - immediately lowers happiness and slightly lowers bond;
  - makes the Petling visibly sulk for about 15 minutes;
  - raises long-term Discipline;
  - lowers Spoiled behaviour;
  - makes future unnecessary demands less frequent.
- GIVE IN:
  - immediately gives the Petling what it asked for (food, petting or play);
  - boosts happiness and appropriate bond/care stats;
  - raises long-term Spoiled behaviour;
  - slightly lowers Discipline;
  - makes future demands increasingly frequent.
- Added persistent Discipline and Spoiled values to saves, with backwards-compatible migration.
- Demand frequency is now dynamically calculated from Discipline vs Spoiled state, ranging from very occasional in a well-behaved Petling to much more frequent in a heavily spoiled one.
- Directly satisfying an existing demand via the normal Feed/Pet/Play routes also teaches the Petling that demanding works, so the system cannot be bypassed.
- Mood text now reflects sulking, demanding, spoiled and well-behaved states.
- Main Petling substatus now shows a simple long-term behaviour label: BALANCED, SPOILED or WELL-BEHAVED.
- This system affects behaviour, not health/death mechanics.
- All Fix 116 health balancing, Fix 115 Penny Pusher work, Day Outs, Quest Director and prior systems are preserved.
