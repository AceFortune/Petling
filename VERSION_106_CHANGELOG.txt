PETLING FIX 106 — SMARTER DAILY QUEST PARSER
- Expanded the Daily Quest free-text parser substantially.
- Questions are now parsed separately from physical/actions commands.
- Asking “what is the item we need?”, “what are we looking for?”, “where do we go?”, etc. no longer accidentally advances the quest or triggers unrelated canned dialogue.
- Added contextual question topics: quest item/relic, objective, missing person, enemy/threat, location, clues/evidence, reward, and quest giver.
- Answers are grounded in the ACTUAL generated quest state: its relic, missing person, locations, current objective, discovered clues, foe, reward and NPC.
- Information the player has not discovered yet is deliberately not spoiled.
- Informational questions do not advance the scene counter.
- Added additional action vocabulary for taking/retrieving/collecting objects.
- Improved parser fallback text so players know the kinds of natural questions and actions the system understands.
- Fix 105 Penny Pusher pressure correction and all previous systems remain intact.
