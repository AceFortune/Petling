PETLING FIX 116 — HEALTH & OVERNIGHT SURVIVAL BALANCE

- Reduced starvation health drain from 5.0/hour to 3.5/hour.
- Reduced severe hygiene/full-poop health drain from about 3.33/hour to about 2.22/hour.
- Reduced exhaustion health drain from 2.5/hour to 1.5/hour.
- Reduced untreated sickness drain from 1.4/hour to 0.9/hour.
- Slightly softened extreme-weight and fragile-start health penalties.
- While asleep, all negative health drain is multiplied by 0.55.
- This makes sleep behave more like a protected recovery period while preserving consequences for prolonged neglect.
- Added a small wake-up safety floor: if the Petling survives the night but would wake below 8 health, it wakes at 8 health rather than being effectively doomed immediately.
- Healthy regeneration remains unchanged.
- Death and neglect mechanics remain active; this is a balance adjustment, not invulnerability.
- Fix 115 Penny Pusher timing/alignment, Fix 114 startup changes, Day Out systems and all prior content are preserved.
