PETLING FIX 107 — QUEST INTERPRETER 2.0
- Rebuilt the daily quest parser around structured intent: actor, action, target, topic, question type, tone and method.
- Added conversational context and follow-up reference handling for him/her/them/it/that/there.
- Added broader questions and meta commands: RECAP, INVENTORY, PEOPLE, LOCATIONS and HELP.
- Added navigation between discovered quest locations.
- Added temporary quest inventory with take, drop, show and give handling.
- Added accusations, deductions/theories, bribery, lying, apologies and comforting approaches.
- Expanded environmental verbs: open, unlock, break, push, pull, burn, cut, dig, climb, swim, jump, knock, throw, read and touch.
- Expanded Petling-directed commands and actor recognition.
- Added lightweight recognition of compound “while” / “then” instructions for future scene logic.
- Quest log now stores structured intent summaries as the adventure progresses.
- Discovered locations and conversational context persist through the current day's quest.
- Existing daily one-quest-per-calendar-day rule remains unchanged: unfinished quests still expire at the date change.
- All prior systems, including Fix 105 Penny Pusher physics, are preserved.
