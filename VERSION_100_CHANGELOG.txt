PETLING FIX 100 — ZERO-FLASH WATER PLACEMENT

- Dynamic water geometry and refraction artwork are unchanged from Fix 99.
- Removed the final visible loading/fallback square completely.
- Water terrain changes now preload every affected dynamic-water tile BEFORE the Garden DOM is updated.
- A single water edit can alter the centre tile plus all eight neighbours, so the full affected 3×3 region is preloaded atomically.
- While those images load, the previous fully-rendered Garden remains on screen.
- Once all required water images are ready, the entire affected area updates together.
