PETLING FIX 105 — PENNY PUSHER PRESSURE FIX
- Fixed the Penny Pusher paying itself out without player input.
- Removed passive forward movement from the entire existing coin field.
- Existing coins now remain stationary unless real pressure reaches them.
- Newly dropped player coins become the source of pushing force when they land.
- Pressure transfers through physical contact chains from coin to coin.
- Valuable gold coins, chips and capsules therefore need the player's inserted coins to physically work them toward the edge.
- Removed the automatic centre/front funnel that was slowly helping prizes fall by themselves.
- The mechanical shelf still visibly cycles so the cabinet remains alive and dynamic.
- Coins hanging over the lip only creep while actual push pressure is being applied.
- Daily Quest system from Fix 104 is preserved unchanged.
